# Runs (game loop) decisions

Moved out of CLAUDE.md word for word (2026-10-26) so it loads only when needed. Everything here is still agreed with the user and binding.

- Agreed with the user: EXP is a separate account currency (earned by beating runs, spent in the post-run shop); ₱ belongs to a run (starts at 500) and goes to `User.bank` as soon as the run ends (beaten or lost). A lost battle costs 1 of 3 HP and the run moves on; a lost gym leader battle is fought again. Each step offers 1–3 encounters (1 about half the time a non-battle encounter is offered; weights 4:2:1); at least 4 steps per run are a required battle (the first step always); a step offering a single trainer and nothing else gets a second trainer to choose between 75% of the time (`PAIR_CHANCE`); steps with a non-battle option are left as rolled, and a run offers at most 2 Pokémon Centers (agreed). Trainer battles lean different ways (`focus`: twice the ₱, twice the EXP, or reward cards one rarity up; placeholder amounts). Items work (`ItemEffect`/`ItemUse` in `items.ts`): equipment is read live on each run action, consumables are used before a battle, the post-run shop stocks 3–4 consumables, 1–2 misc items and 1 piece of gear after each run (`shopStock`), never a one-of-a-kind item (`onlyOne`) the account already has, and the stock is fixed on the run (`Run.shop`) once the shop first opens, battle effects become the player's battle rules. The user said to balance them later, so prices and strengths are placeholders.
- Trainers use the CPU's Trainer difficulty with `generateDeck`; elite (Ace) trainers and bosses use Gym leader difficulty. Every gym leader has a required Gym Trainer (their gym's type, Trainer difficulty) on the step before them, except in the Gym Challenge and Champion runs (agreed): a Gym Challenge is the 8 gym leaders back to back with a Pokémon Center after the fourth; a Champion run the 8 gym leaders, the Elite Four and the Champion back to back (Kanto and Johto alike; `gymTrainers: false`, `center` on `RunSegment`).

- Gen 2 (agreed, scaffolding; card effects to come later, Neo Genesis's, Neo Discovery's, Southern Islands', Neo Revelation's, Neo Destiny's, Expedition's, Aquapolis's and Skyridge's done): sets Neo Genesis → Skyridge with Southern Islands, not Legendary Collection, which is Gen 1's (agreed; `GEN2_SETS`); Johto runs (8 gyms, Johto Gym Challenge, Johto Champion) with bosses in `johto-bosses.ts` (our own decks of Gen 2 cards around each character's GSC/HGSS team; ids `johto-koga`/`johto-bruno`/`johto-lance`); random decks use `GEN2_DECK_RULES` (Neo Genesis basic Energy, Gen 2 Trainers, no Double Colorless); Johto badges and the Johto Champion Ribbon; boss sprites from HeartGold/SoulSilver (the decompilation's GSLEADER, GSBIGFOUR and WATARU overworld sprites, by hgss_sprites.py). The run picker has a Generation dropdown; the Packs tab shows every set with a multi-select generation filter (`GenerationFilter`).
- Conceding (agreed): only while choosing the next step; the run is lost (`conceded`, no retry, no EXP; its consumables still in effect wait for the next run with the battles they have left, as on a run lost on HP, `carriedConsumables`, the user's; a conceded battle still uses one of a consumable's battles; Battle Tower battles never use consumables), its gained cards stay unlocked. However a run ends, its ₱ are banked only once a battle has been completed without conceding that game, won or lost (`battlesCompleted`, `bankablePokedollars`; the API passes `conceded` with `BATTLE_OVER` from the game's action rows).
- Progress (agreed): an account starts with no booster packs. A run's first completed battle (same rule) unlocks its generation's next pack in release order (`GENERATIONS`, `nextPack`; `PackUnlock`, `apps/api/src/progress.ts`), beating a run all of the generation's packs. Gen 2 runs, when they exist, unlock from Neo Genesis. Gen 1's gym runs are open from the start; every run of a later generation needs the previous generation's Gym Challenge beaten (agreed 2026-10-24: Kanto's opens Johto's 8 gym runs; `generationLock`, its generation not pickable in the run picker's dropdown until then); the Gym Challenge needs all its generation's badges, the Champion run its Gym Challenge beaten (`runTypeLock`). The run picker shows each run's final boss's overworld sprite (`public/trainers/`, from the remake where there is one: FireRed/LeafGreen for Kanto, via the pret decompilation) and ticks runs beaten.
- Unlocks (agreed): a card gained in a run is unlocked at once (`CardUnlock`), never added to the run's deck (trades excepted; and on any map step, battle or not, the run's deck can be switched for another of the account's playable decks, in the `RouteChoice` panel, as the option is taken); unlocks are per card, not per copy, except that a holo with a non-holo version of the same card in its set (`holoTwins`) is a special addition: it counts every copy gained (`CardUnlock.quantity`) and unlocks the non-holo version with it; holo-only cards (Base Set Alakazam) are ordinary unlocks with 4 copies; holo Energy counts copies too, but is never counted in the numbers of cards collected (the Packs tab's unlocked count, the Collection's card count; a deck's size still counts it); a deck holds no more copies of a special holo or a holo Energy than were gained (the starter deck uses Fossil's non-holo Lapras for this); basic Energy is always unlocked (holo Energy isn't: found only in packs, 4% of Energy cards (agreed 2026-10-23; 2% before), one random holo-library style each, and a deck holds no more of each than were pulled: `CardUnlock.quantity`); a Base Set 2 card and the earlier print with its look are unlocked together, either way round, whenever neither is holo (commons, uncommons and non-holo rares; holos look different and unlock separately; only Base Set 2, no other set); Base Set 2's holo rares are shown as galaxy foil; a new account has no run and no deck (the user's, 2026-10-25): its first run picks one of three starter decks (`STARTER_DECKS`: Water & Lightning, the old one; Fire & Fighting; Grass & Psychic; never Blastoise, the user's), saved to the account's deck list with its cards unlocked (`run.start`'s `starter`, `needsStarter`). Runs start with one of the account's decks, which must follow the rules and hold only unlocked cards. Run deck (agreed): during a run the deck list has an automatic, read-only "Current run" deck (`Deck.runId`) copying the run's deck; when the run is over it can be saved under a name (default "Run #N – <run type>"), and if it isn't, the next run (started with one of the account's own decks) takes it over. Locked cards are darker on the Collection tab, and shown without holo effects. (The Cards tab was removed: the deck builder covers browsing cards.)
- Booster packs (agreed): bought with banked ₱ on the Packs tab, one per set with card effects; 11 cards with averaged WOTC-era odds (1 rare, holo 1 in 3; 3 uncommons; 6 commons, each of the 9 rolling to be an extra rare: 4%, then 2%, then sharply less as the pack gets more; 0.6% of packs all rares, with fireworks; 1 basic Energy: the set's own or the closest earlier set's of its generation, sometimes holo). Every card opened is unlocked, and one already obtained gives Stardust (agreed; new unlocks give none): 50 a common, 100 an uncommon, 300 a rare or holo; crafting a card in the deck builder's right-click view costs 100 (common/uncommon) or 500 (rare/holo) and unlocks it as a pull would (only cards packs hold, not basic or holo Energy, and only from sets whose packs are unlocked).
- Cosmetics (agreed 2026-10-22): card backs (the player's own face-down cards) and playmats (both sides of the table, the CPU's in the player's playmat too; on a phone the whole battle screen's background, agreed 2026-10-23; faint geometric patterns answering a click subtly: shifting a few pixels or lighting lines and points, never turning or flipping shapes), and coins (tossed for every coin flip: a 3D coin flicked up with a burst of streaks, landing with a puff of dust, agreed 2026-10-23), picked on the Cosmetics tab (after Inventory; agreed); some sold for banked ₱ (placeholder prices), some unlocked by beating a Gym Challenge or Champion run, all unlocked in debug mode (`cosmetics.ts` in campaign, `routers/cosmetics.ts`). The card backs are the official English and Japanese backs, versions of them with the holo library's textures, and Pokémon TCG Pocket's card sleeves (most for ₱, two behind each Gym Challenge and Champion run; agreed). Each cosmetic the account has is assigned to any number of decks, on the Cosmetics tab only (Assign in place of Buy opens a searchable, scrolling multi-select of the decks with "Default for decks without one", the tile keeping just its Assign button; agreed 2026-10-23), and used in those decks' battles; a deck with none of a kind, and everything outside battles, uses the account's default of that kind.
