Moved out of CLAUDE.md word for word (2026-10-26) so it loads only when needed. Everything here is still agreed with the user and binding.

- The site menu, dashboard style (`NavRail`): on a big screen a column of the pages' icons down the left edge under the top bar, the current page lit in yellow (each page's icon a pixel-art sprite in `public/nav/`, agreed 2026-10-23), Settings (`/settings`, `SETTINGS_PAGE`) under Extras (the last page; Training is one of Play's modes, not a page, and Play is lit on its menu, its modes and their battles: `usePlayLit`); the ☰ toggles it open, static in the page's flow: wider, each page's name beside its icon, Play's modes in an accordion under Play (`PlayModes`, the user's, 2026-10-25; in the phone's ☰ menu too, not the minimised rail), taking room on the left with the page beside it (not dimmed, still usable), remembered (`poke-tcg.nav-open`). During a run the run's numbers (`RunStats`, `NavRun`) are in it, under the pages with a margin (minimised: HP and ₱; open: only HP, the run's EXP and ₱, no items in effect or badges won, agreed 2026-10-24); the account's bank, EXP and Stardust are always in the top bar on the right, before the music player's bar, run or not (`TopBarAccount`, the user's), instead of a Run tab in the big screen's side panels (phones keep the Run tab). Under it, the logs (Run log / Battle log tabs at the top, and Debug in a game with the debug tools on; the game page passes its log up with `useGameLogInNav`); the run section is as tall as its numbers, the log's top right under the run's ₱ (the user's, 2026-10-24; it was dragged to a height before), and the log fills the rest, its bottom anchored to the menu's bottom (the log tab remembered, `poke-tcg.nav-sections`). On a phone there's no column: the ☰ opens the menu as a `SidePanel` from the left, with the music player's bar.
- The account's email is never shown on its own (agreed). The top bar has no account menu (agreed 2026-10-23): the account name is changed on the Settings page (`AccountNameField`). On a phone the top bar shows the run's HP and ₱ (during a run), the bank and Stardust (`TopBarStats`, agreed 2026-10-23).

## UI and interaction decisions (game board)
- The game page fills the window. Side margins come from `PAGE_WIDTH` in `apps/web/src/utils/layout.ts`, shared with the Decks page.
- Card sizes are CSS variables (`--card-sm`/`--card-lg`) on `.play-area-cards` in `index.css`. They scale with the play area's container size, and the large card is 1.4× the small one. Use `SMALL_CARD`/`LARGE_CARD`/`SIDE_COLUMN` rather than hard-coded sizes.
- Hand (`Hand.tsx`):
  - Docked at the bottom edge and partly hidden.
  - A hovered card grows and lifts while its neighbours slide aside, and every card keeps at least a small peek.
  - At rest it stays inside the play area width; it only spills into the margins while a card is hovered.
- Playing cards:
  - Drag cards onto slots. Trainers can be dropped anywhere on the play area.
  - The board glows green for a valid drop and red when the drop would cancel. Dropping on the hand cancels.
- Any effect that asks for a Pokémon is answered by clicking a highlighted slot on the board, not by picking from a list. Effects that pick cards from the hand do it in the hand itself.
- Benched Pokémon Powers, discarding Clefairy Doll and using the Stadium (Lucky Stadium, `StadiumCard`, which pulses with the playable cards' blue outline when it can be used) are done by clicking the card. A card whose only option is its effect uses it at once, with no menu or confirm button; when it can't be used, a warning toast says why. Except (agreed 2026-10-23), on every screen: clicking your Active Pokémon opens a panel on the card with its attacks, Pokémon Power, Retreat and other moves (replacing the column beside it), and clicking a benched Pokémon with a power opens a panel on it with the power's name and a Use button (or why it can't be used; not its text, agreed 2026-10-23); `cardMenu` in Board.tsx. Pokémon whose power can be used pulse like playable cards, and so does your Active Pokémon when it can attack (agreed 2026-10-24).
- Clicking outside cancels an in-progress move:
  - The listener uses the capture phase.
  - Elements marked `data-keep-action` are exempt.
  - Clicks right after a drag are ignored.
- Dialogs share one style: `bg-slate-800`, `ring-1 ring-slate-600`, buttons `bg-slate-600`/`hover:bg-slate-500`.
  - The Trainer dialog floats above the CPU's Active Pokémon. It shows no card image, and an eye button makes it see-through and click-through. It only appears when it has something to click (buttons, a multi-card Choose); single-click questions are a prompt toast, and an unplayable Trainer is a warning toast.
  - The deck-search dialog is large and centred, and is rendered outside the PlayArea (its `container-type` would trap fixed elements).
- Inventory (agreed): page width like the other tabs; items show their picture (`ItemIcon`, `public/items/`, game item icons or the closest stand-in) in the Inventory and the shop; equipment is equipped by dragging (or click, then click) a piece from the grid onto the slots, a 2×3 grid in the top left corner of the item grid's column (the pieces wrapping around it), beside the account's trainer sprite (any of Showdown's ~1,500, `TRAINER_SPRITES`, hotlinked; `User.trainerSprite`, default Hilbert; picked with Change trainer under it) (the sprite a quarter of the row; the slots and the grid three quarters) (`EquipmentPanel`); beside Change trainer, a drop-down of the player's sprite on the run maps, the boy (Red, Ethan, Brendan) or the girl (Leaf, Lyra, May) (`User.playerGender`), the slots it fits green and the others red while it's held. Key items show a badge case per generation (`BadgeCases`) instead of each badge; it opens to the generation's badges, the ones not yet won greyed out.
- The music player (`MusicPlayer`: its bar at the right end of the top bar on a big screen (the user's, replacing the bottom-right bar and the battle table's), in the site's ☰ menu on a phone and in the phone battle screen's ☰ menu, agreed so it never covers the hand; KHInsider streams listed in `public/music/tracks.json`, gens 1–5, each game in its region's generation, not its release's (FireRed & LeafGreen Gen 1, HeartGold & SoulSilver Gen 2, Omega Ruby & Alpha Sapphire Gen 3; agreed 2026-10-23), tracks of 1 minute or more, battle music listed apart) was agreed with the user, licensing aside: the app isn't public. Auto play (agreed 2026-10-23; `runMusic` in campaign's `music.ts`, README: Music): loops the route's theme on a run's map, the town's over the last 3 steps before the gym, the gym's inside it and the League's in the Elite Four's and Champion's rooms; a trainer's battle (Gym and Ace Trainers' too) one of the trainer, wild Pokémon and rival themes at random, gym leaders, Elite Four and Champions their own; the Pokémon Center theme in a center, and the healing jingle as Nurse Joy heals, then back to the song where it was; Training battles too, other pages untouched; songs fade into each other, every battle starts its song from the beginning, the Viridian City run plays Rocket Hideout, and a beaten run plays its region's victory theme once, then shuffles the album's non-battle music (agreed 2026-10-24); the soundtrack Original or Remastered on the Settings page (auto play on and Remastered by default, agreed 2026-10-23). Each map's and town's song is the games' own (the decompilations' map data), not guessed.
- Settings follow the account (agreed 2026-10-23): the Settings page's choices (theme, font, coin flip animations, played-card options, reminders, music auto play and soundtrack, Themed EXP bar and Themed Pokédollars; an on/off one is made with `toggleSetting`) are kept in `User.settings` (`settings` router) as well as localStorage; a new one must be added to `ACCOUNT_SETTINGS` and its store wired through `registerSetting`/`saveSetting` (`utils/accountSettings.ts`). README: Account settings.
- Hints and errors are toasts (`useToasts`, bottom-right, 4s, deduped), not inline text. Instructions for a move in progress are `Toasts`' `prompt`: shown while the move is pending, gone the instant it's confirmed or cancelled, no timer.
- Run screen (agreed 2026-10-22): on a big screen the run's numbers and the run log are in the site menu, so the panel below the route map has no tabs; it's always two columns, the step's encounters on the left (battles, then the other stops) and the deck choice on the right (on every step, battle or not).
- Side panel tabs (`LogDrawer.tsx`: Run, the log, Options, Debug; the tab remembered under `poke-tcg.game-log`): on a phone only (`LogSheet`, `PanelTabs`). A battle on a big screen has no ☰ menu (removed 2026-10-22): the logs and Debug are in the site menu, the options on the Settings page. The window-edge `LogDrawer` panel was removed (2026-10-22): on a big screen the logs are in the site menu.
- High-res card images are used for the hand, the Active Pokémon and the drag overlay; small images elsewhere.
- Cards show the large hover preview (`CardPreview`, via `PreviewContext`) everywhere except the hand, which grows the hovered card instead. Outside the board, wrap the page in `PreviewProvider`.
- Right-clicking any card, anywhere, opens the `CardInspector` (large, all holo effects; `CardInspectProvider` around every page, the deck builder adding its − and +). New card elements should use `useInspectOnRightClick`.
- The Active Pokémon shows its holo effect when it's a holo, but still (`HoloCard` `still`): no tilt or reaction to the pointer. Its slot animations (drop-in, shake, lunge, glows) do tilt and shine it with them (`HoloMotion`), then it settles back.

## Phone layout (agreed)
- Phones held upright (`useIsPhone`) get their own battle screen and Collection layout; tablets and desktops keep the usual ones. See README: Phone layout.
- Battle screen: locked full screen (`useLockedScreen`), TCG Pocket style (`components/phone/`); tap your Active for its moves, in a panel on the card as on a big screen (agreed 2026-10-23; the slide-up sheet was removed); the piles right of each Active (agreed 2026-10-23): the Prize stack above the deck, the discard pile 1.5× as big to their left, centred on them (the Stadium in the bar, 1.3× the piles, centred with its other things); both Actives the same size (1.36–1.68× a Bench card, agreed); no top line (who the CPU is, its items, its hand size): the CPU's hand face down in a strip at the top, above its Bench (never covering it), instead, a tap on it showing its card count briefly; Start battle centred with Reset cards on its right; the turn and setup banners (both layouts, agreed 2026-10-24) a band across the play area centred on the buttons between the sides and behind them, the text split either side ("Your/Opponent's Turn" | the turn count, "You're going first/second." | "Place your Basic Pokémon."; on a phone the setup line's halves above and below the buttons instead, agreed 2026-10-24), each half on its own band fading out at the Active slot and centred on it, both one size at 57% of the band's height, each half sliding in from its own side, then the band fading away (the setup one on Start battle); each player's coins flipped in the space left of their Active, the coin 30% smaller; the hand 70% showing; the round ☰ button opens the site's pages at the top (`PageMenu`, a row of icon-only buttons; agreed, since the battle screen covers the header) and the side panel's tabs and Concede below (`LogSheet`). No hover preview: a long press inspects a card.
- Phone run screen: the map has a lock button under Centre on you and the speed button (agreed): locked (the default), the map doesn't pan and a swipe over it scrolls the page.
- Phone run screen: the encounter choice (the compact one every screen has, agreed) heads the other stops "Event" and makes the decks a drop-down menu, "Unlocked this run" starts minimised, and the Run / Run log / Options tabs are in the panel under the deck choice (or under the encounter under way; `PanelTabs`, 24rem tall, collapsible to its tab row, remembered); during a run the run's numbers and log are also in the site's ☰ panel under the pages, as on a big screen (agreed 2026-10-23; the floating button in the top right corner is gone).
- Phone Collection: a swipe to the right opens the decks panel (the open deck's cards); a panel swiped back towards its edge closes. Opening a saved deck from the list keeps the panel open (a new deck closes it). The card view (`CardInspector`) has a × and closes on a tap anywhere but the card and its controls.
- Phone hand: 70% showing at rest (30% below the edge); a tap brings the card up whole and enlarged (down again on a tap off it) and selects it, the cards beside it making way so it never covers one (agreed 2026-10-23); hold zooms in (large, above the hand) and sliding along the hand changes the zoomed card; sliding up off the hand drags the zoomed card into play. A long press on a hand card never opens the CardInspector (agreed 2026-10-23); cards in play still do.
- Favorite decks (agreed): the star in a deck tile's top right corner on the Collection tab (`Deck.favorite`); a run's deck choice is a drop-down on every screen with the favorite decks under it. Deck tiles show the card count at the bottom right, its colour meaning ready.
- Favorites (agreed): the ★ in a card's large view adds it to the account's favorites (`FavoriteCard`); the search's Favorites toggle (before Pokémon) is the only Favorites view (a separate list was tried and removed). The site's "Single battle" tab is called Training (Practice until 2026-10-23).
- Collection: the cards fill the screen; the decks (compact tiles) and the search are side panels slid in by the Decks and Filters buttons above them.
- Packs: a grid 4 wide by 2 tall.

## Deck sharing (agreed)
- Share codes (`PTCG1.…`, `apps/api/src/deck-codes.ts`) and Community Decks (the tab after Collection, before Extras) hold each card's non-holo version when it has one; importing uses as many of the importer's own holos as they have, the rest non-holo.
- Imports keep locked cards. Any deck may hold locked cards (the builder adds them with a warning, the API saves them); such a deck can't be played, and wherever deck tiles are shown it's greyed out and tagged Locked (`LOCKED_TAG`).
- Community Decks tiles are one size, descriptions cut short with an ellipsis; clicking one focuses it (a little larger, the whole description, and only then its Import, Copy code, Cards and Unpublish buttons); clicking it again, outside or Escape puts it back.
- A published community deck is a snapshot (later edits don't change it; its author can unpublish it), only of a deck that follows the rules, shown with the author's account name (`User.accountName`: chosen by them, not unique, required to publish), never their Google name (`displayName`) or email.

## Post-run screen (agreed)
- A beaten run's screen: the shop first, then the cards gained (with the results), then the next run. The same on every screen size.
