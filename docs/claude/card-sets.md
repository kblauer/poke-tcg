# Card sets: per-set status and rulings (Gen 1–3)

Moved out of CLAUDE.md word for word (2026-10-26) so it loads only when needed. Everything here is still agreed with the user and binding.

- Team Rocket (base5) is done (`effects/rocket.ts`, `rocket-powers.ts`, `rocket-trainers.ts`, rulings in their headers) and in the run pool and packs; the gym leader CPU plays them through its generic judgement. The campaign's test pool (`test-pool.ts`) copies the API's card fixes (Dark Stage 2 evolutions, printed attack costs, special Energy), so keep the two in step. Rainbow Energy is `'any'` in an energy pool: check attached Energy of a type with `counts`, `isEnergyCardOf` or `payCost` (engine.ts), never by comparing `energyType`. Its Dark Stage 2 Pokémon evolve from the Dark Stage 1 (fixed in `apps/api/src/cards.ts`).
- Legendary Collection (lc) is done (`effects/lc.ts`) and in Gen 1's pool and packs (agreed): its cards reprint Base Set, Jungle, Fossil, Team Rocket and promo cards (`LC_REPRINTS`, as Base Set 2's do); LC Haunter's Transparency stops with any Special Condition. TCGdex's LC damage and cost gaps are fixed in `lc.ts`; Nidoran's spacing ("Nidoran ♀", in the e-Card sets too) and two misspelt attacks in the seed (re-seed).
- Base Set 2 is done (`effects/base4.ts`) and in the run pool. Its cards are all reprints: each takes its original's attacks, power and Trainer effect through `BASE4_REPRINTS` and `base4Reprints`, so fixing an original fixes its reprint too. Never key behaviour on a specific card id (use names, as the CPUs do), or the reprint won't match.
- Gym Heroes (gym1) is done on the `gym-heroes-sets` branch (`effects/gym1.ts`, `gym1-powers.ts`, `gym1-trainers.ts`, rulings in their headers) and in the run pool and packs. Agreed: Bench Guard and Vermilion City Gym ask each time (the CPU always flips), Flee is asked before the attack, Crosscounter applies Weakness and Resistance, Celadon City Gym can be used any number of times. Choices that must wait behind another go in `GameState.queuedChoices`. Bench room goes through `openBenchSlot` (Narrow Gym).
- Gym Challenge (gym2) is done on the same branch (`effects/gym2.ts`, `gym2-powers.ts`, `gym2-trainers.ts`, rulings in their headers) and in the run pool and packs. Agreed: Rocket's Minefield Gym puts 2 damage counters on tails, Chaos Gym's tails lets the opponent play the card, Blaine's Quiz #3 offers 4 names, Shapeshift and Secret Plan have their full rules. A face-down card (Secret Plan) must never reach the opponent's view (`getPlayerView` hides it); anything that damages or evolves a Pokémon must turn it over first (`revealFaceDown`). Shapeshift's card is an `attached` entry, so it leaves play with its Pokémon. Owners' Pokémon evolve only from the same owner's (`OWNER` in `apps/api/src/cards.ts`, copied in `test-pool.ts`); never key an owner check on anything but the name. The gym leader CPU plays most of its Trainers (README: The CPU opponent); the Trainer CPU stays as documented (agreed).
- Neo Genesis (neo1) is done: attacks, Pokémon Powers, Trainers and special Energy (`effects/neo1.ts`, `neo1-powers.ts`, `neo1-trainers.ts` with `NEO1_ENERGY`, rulings in their headers). Its cards are in Gen 2 (Johto) runs and Neo Genesis packs only, never Kanto runs' pool (agreed; Gen 2's pool takes only implemented cards from `GEN2_DONE_SETS`, every card of the other Gen 2 sets); any playable deck can still start any run (agreed). Pokémon Tools are `attached` entries with `tool` (one per Pokémon); a Tool that works by itself logs it with `logTool` (its Pokémon and what it did), which the board animates between the attack and the turn's end (agreed 2026-10-24: a Berry's green "+20", `ToolPops`); anything that discards the opponent's Energy must go through `discardTheirEnergy` (Ecogym). Berries work automatically between turns; Time Capsule's CPU takes Pokémon first. Agreed: Pilfer works with no Bench (its player then loses); Static Electricity counts every Mareep in play, both players', for the attacking Mareep; Jaw Clamp and Sticky Nectar stop any effect, whoever's, from changing the held player's Active (`activeLock`), which can't retreat either. Mind Games asks Slowking's owner each time (the CPU always flips; a flip per Slowking); Wild Growth doesn't stack; Final Blow and Hydroelectric Power are asked before the attack. TCGdex lists the Neo babies as Basic: they're in `BABIES` (apps/api/src/cards.ts).
- Neo Discovery (neo2) is done on the `neo-discovery-sets` branch (built on `more-UI-improvements`): attacks, Pokémon Powers and Trainers (`effects/neo2.ts`, `neo2-powers.ts`, `neo2-trainers.ts`, rulings in their headers); 21–36 are the holo rares again (`withNonHolo`, `neo2-prints.ts`). Agreed: Pineco's Burst flips twice (the second tails only stops the 40 to the Defending Pokémon); Counter does back the damage taken without Weakness or Resistance; Hatch and Shockwave do their "Then…" part only on heads; Energy Evolution works on any Energy card its owner attaches to Eevee, by any effect (checked in `applyAction`), at any time, Rainbow Energy giving any Eeveelution. Spikes is checked by `newActives` (with every Knock Out check). Every Unown limits a deck to 4 Basic Pokémon with Unown in their names (`MAX_UNOWN`, packages/shared/src/deck.ts). A power's `name` overrides TCGdex's (the Unowns' bracketed names).
- Southern Islands (si1, `effects/si1.ts`) and Neo Revelation (neo3, `effects/neo3.ts`, `neo3-powers.ts`, `neo3-trainers.ts`, rulings in their headers) are done and in `GEN2_DONE_SETS`. Southern Islands Marill's and Tentacruel's attacks are fixed in the seed's `TCGDEX_FIXES` (a re-seed is needed for Marill's); the API keeps each attack name once. Agreed: Legendary Body keeps every Trainer card, either player's, but Stadiums from affecting the Active Pokémon and its Energy, and discards Trainer cards attached to it; any Trainer effect that reaches an Active Pokémon must check `trainerProof` (Trainers' Pokémon and Energy questions do it for you). Pokémon Breeder Fields finds any later stage of the line. Allergic Pollen: anything an attack or power does with a discard pile checks `discardsLocked`; Unown K's Keep: any discard of the opponent's Energy checks `energyKept`. A basic Energy card can be another type for the turn (`CardInstance.converted`): check Energy types with `energyProvided` or `isEnergyCardOf`.
- Neo Destiny (neo4, `effects/neo4.ts`, `neo4-powers.ts`, `neo4-trainers.ts` with Miracle Energy, rulings in their headers) is done and in `GEN2_DONE_SETS`. Light Stage 2 Pokémon evolve from the Light Stage 1 (`evolvesFrom` in apps/api/src/cards.ts, copied in `test-pool.ts`). Shining Pokémon and Miracle Energy are 1 to a deck (`onlyOnePerDeck`, packages/shared/src/deck.ts). Shining Tyranitar's attacks come from the seed's `TCGDEX_FIXES` (re-seed). Agreed (as Hatch): Explosive Evolution and Sunbathe do their "Then…" part only on heads. Miraculous Wind marks Special Energy `windless`: anything that reads a Special Energy card's effect (Darkness, Metal, Rainbow, Recycle…) must skip `windless` cards.
- Expedition (ecard1, `effects/ecard1.ts`, `ecard1-powers.ts`, `ecard1-trainers.ts`, rulings in their headers) is done and in `GEN2_DONE_SETS` (`packages/campaign/src/ecard.test.ts` checks every e-Card card is implemented). Agreed: Bill's Maintenance can't be played with no other card in the hand. TCGdex's e-Card data is unreliable: check every card against its image (Weakness, Resistance, "Evolves from", damage, attack names) and fix it in the seed's `TCGDEX_FIXES` (re-seed). Poké-Powers stop with any Special Condition (as every Pokémon Power does), Poké-Bodies never do (`ignoresConditions`). Supporters are `supporter: true` (one a turn). A Technical Machine is a Trainer with `technicalMachine` (an attack, in `usableAttacks`); every caller of `usableAttacks` must pass the opponent's Bench (`oppBench`) for Psymimic. Choice fields added for it: `ownTo` (attacks) and `fromSide`/`toSide` (powers), in the shared schemas too.
- Aquapolis (ecard2, `effects/ecard2.ts`, `ecard2-powers.ts`, `ecard2-trainers.ts` with `ECARD2_ENERGY`, rulings in their headers) is done and in `GEN2_DONE_SETS`. Crystal Energy is a `crystal:<types>` member of the energy pool: count Energy with `counts`/`payCost`, never by comparing pool entries. Dark Impact's `noPokePowers` stops only Poké-Powers (not bodies, `body: true`). Retreat-cost changes go through `retreatBodies`, Pokémon Tool rules live where they apply (Healing Berry in `useBerry`, Memory Berry in `usableAttacks`, Time Shard in `knockOut`). Agreed: Dark Impact (and Dark Gaze) stop the older sets' Pokémon Powers too; Crystal Energy is one Energy counting as any one attached basic type; Time Shard's owner picks up to 2 when more than 2 basic Energy are attached.
- Skyridge (ecard3, `effects/ecard3.ts`, `ecard3-powers.ts`, `ecard3-trainers.ts` with `ECARD3_ENERGY`, rulings in their headers) is done and in `GEN2_DONE_SETS`. Anything that evolves a Pokémon must accept Buried Fossil for Mysterious Fossil evolutions (`evolvesOnto`), and anything that gives a Special Condition must check `conditionProof` (Clefairy Doll, Buried Fossil). Poké-Bodies must be checked with `powerActive` so Ancient Wind turns them off. Skyridge's pending choices are in `skyridgeView` and `resolveSkyridge` (trainers.ts); reused attacks from other sets go through `same(() => …)` in ecard3.ts, as the effect tables can't be read while the modules load. Agreed: Star Piece picks the first matching card by itself and stays attached when nothing evolves; Streaming Mantle's players pick their 3 basic Energy cards when they have more than 3; Lonely Fang and Magnetic Wave never go below 30; Evolution Helper isn't stopped by Primal Aura or Primal Stare; Investigate's player picks which Prize cards; Retro Cave and Healing Oasis ask only at exactly 2 Prizes.
- Generation 3 (EX sets, `ex1`–`ex16`, `EX_SETS`): in the catalog, Gen 3's (Hoenn's) runs' cards and packs (`GENERATIONS[3]`, `GEN3_SETS`, `GEN3_DECK_RULES`; its packs unlock by its runs, as the other generations'; see the Hoenn runs bullet under Runs). Reverse holos (agreed): collectible prints of their own (`<card id>-reverse`, made by the seed, `reverseHoloCard`), special additions like holo twins (copies counted, deck limited, unlocking the base print), from packs (one in place of a common), run rewards and crafting, hidden on the Collection tab until gained and never counted in the numbers of cards collected, like holo Energy (agreed; `isVersion`); anything keyed on a card id must accept the suffix (the engine's effect lookups strip it). Don't re-seed the shared database until this code is on main: older code's `toCardDef` throws on Pokémon-ex with no stage. Pokémon-ex are `ex: true` (2 Prize cards). EX Ruby & Sapphire (`effects/ex1.ts`, `ex1-powers.ts`, `ex1-trainers.ts`) is done. Agreed (2026-10-21): "each Defending Pokémon" is the Active Pokémon only (changed 2026-10-22; at first the Bench too, without Weakness or Resistance; Special Conditions only on the Active one); "the other Defending Pokémon" is a Benched one picked by the player whose card moves; any other "Active Pokémon" is the one Active; "Energy attached" counts Energy provided (Double Colorless 2), "not used to pay" is that less the cost; Lazy stops older Pokémon Powers; Psy Shadow may Knock Out; Water Call sets off Natural Remedy. Darkness and Metal Energy play by the EX rules for every set (agreed): Darkness +10 to the Active before Weakness and Resistance only from Darkness or Dark Pokémon, no damage counters; Metal −10 after Weakness and Resistance only on Metal Pokémon. The EX holo masks (`data-era="ex"`, `data-pokemon-ex`, wotc.css) are measured on a few cards and need checking by eye. Attacks of ex2–ex16 worded like other cards' are played by shared templates (`effects/ex-templates.ts`, matched by `scripts/generate-ex-templates.ts` into `ex-template-data.ts`; re-run it after changing a template or the snapshot); a set's own effects file overrides them. Add a template only for a wording whose rules are settled; a new one needing a ruling is asked like any card. EX Sandstorm (`effects/ex2.ts`, `ex2-powers.ts`, `ex2-trainers.ts`) and EX Dragon (`effects/ex3.ts`, `ex3-powers.ts`, `ex3-trainers.ts`) are done (README: their agreed rulings). Agreed (2026-10-21), for every EX set: "all Defending Pokémon" is the opponent's Active and Benched Pokémon; a partner "in play" is its owner's own; a named Pokémon matches every variant of it (owners', Dark, Light, Shining, δ, ☆: `namesMatch`, every set's named searches too) but a Pokémon-ex never matches its non-ex name; the older sets' Pokémon Powers count as Poké-Powers. Anything that checks a Pokémon's type must use `hasType`/`typesOf` (Kecleon's Energy Variation and printed dual types make it several). EX Team Magma vs Team Aqua (`effects/ex4*.ts`) and EX Hidden Legends (`effects/ex5*.ts`) are done (README: their agreed rulings). Agreed (2026-10-21), for every set: a second printed Weakness or Resistance counts but applies once; evolution follows the printed "Evolves from" exactly (variants never evolve into each other); only δ Pokémon share their base Pokémon's 4-copy deck limit (`limitName`); "an owner in its name" is Team Aqua's/Magma's, Gym Heroes owners' and Rocket's (`hasOwner`); evolving from the deck by an attack or a Belt keeps the usual limits. TCGdex has no "Evolves from" for EX Team Magma vs Team Aqua's evolutions (filled in by the seed, from the printed cards) and no attacks on Technical Machines: check later sets' images for both. EX FireRed & LeafGreen (`effects/ex6*.ts`) and EX Team Rocket Returns (`effects/ex7*.ts`) are done (README: their agreed rulings). Agreed (2026-10-22): "the/each Defending Pokémon" is the opponent's Active Pokémon only, only "all Defending Pokémon" reaches the Bench (every set's, the earlier ones changed too); "discard N Energy" counts cards, "Energy attached" counts Energy provided; powers whose counters add up do (Retreat Aid, Power Gene), one Darkest Impulse however many; a Pokémon that takes another's place (Form Variation, Swoop! Teleporter) keeps everything, can evolve if the old one could and can use its Poké-Power. Image checks (agreed 2026-10-22): check a card's image only where a script finds the data suspect (empty powers, damage that doesn't match its text, attack costs that don't fit the Pokémon's type, repeated types, missing stage or "Evolves from"), not every card. Body checks between turns go through the `checkup` hook; a Stadium's attack through `stadiumAttack` (callers of `usableAttacks` pass `stadium`). EX Deoxys (`effects/ex8*.ts`) and EX Emerald (`effects/ex9*.ts`) are done (README: their agreed rulings). Agreed (2026-10-22): Dragon Dance and Bay Dance are for whichever Pokémon is Active next turn, never the Bench; "your Pokémon" protections from the Active Pokémon (Intimidating Pattern, Lazy Aura) cover the Bench too; Hard Protection covers recoil and Confusion. A "[type] Energy card" in an effect is basic or Special of that type: check with `energyCardOf` (ex2.ts), not `isEnergyCardOf` (basic only). Stadiums that switch powers off set `stadiumMuted` in refreshHideout. EX Unseen Forces (`effects/ex10*.ts`) and EX Delta Species (`effects/ex11*.ts`) are done (README: their agreed rulings). Agreed (2026-10-22): Energy Evolution (EX) gives any type the Energy card provides; Golden Wing moves up to 2 cards; Delta Moon works as Darkest Impulse; Holon Energy GL protects only its own Pokémon, after Weakness and Resistance; Holon's Pokémon aren't owners. A δ Pokémon is one whose name ends " δ". "Once a turn, whichever card" limits (Duplicate, Delta Heal, Snappy Move) go in `PlayerState.powerTurns`; an attack whose cost changes uses `costNow`; anything a Pokémon-ex can't use (Holon Energy) checks `holonWith`. EX Legend Maker (`effects/ex12*.ts`) and EX Holon Phantoms (`effects/ex13*.ts`) are done (README: their agreed rulings). Agreed (2026-10-22): a Holon's Pokémon can be attached as a Special Energy card (`holonEnergy`, cards.ts), using the turn's attachment; an Energy card that provides a type counts as "a [type] Energy" for effects that trigger on attaching it; "a Pokémon that has a Poké-Power/Poké-Body" is the printed power, working or not; evolving from the deck by a Poké-Power ignores the first-turn limit but is the evolution for the turn; the newer Fossils are Trainer cards in play too (`asPokemon.alsoTrainer`). Auras that stop powers go in `auraStops`, checking the target before the source works; React Energy is checked with `hasReact`/`reactCount`. EX Crystal Guardians (`effects/ex14*.ts`), EX Dragon Frontiers (`effects/ex15*.ts`) and EX Power Keepers (`effects/ex16*.ts`) are done, so every EX set is (README: their agreed rulings). Agreed (2026-10-22): a Special Energy card "providing 2 or more" counts what it provides at the time; markers (Imprison, Shock-wave) go on Benched Pokémon too and stay until they leave play; a "the turn ends" power ends it however much it did; Holon Veil's δ counts for both players' cards; Chlorophyll changes any card providing only Colorless Energy, keeping the amount. A card with both a Poké-Body and a Poké-Power has the body as its passive effect and `usePoke` on its `use`. Anything that checks for a δ Pokémon must use `isDelta`/`isDeltaCard` (Holon Veil); anything that reads a card's Energy one by one must go through `cardEnergy` (Crystal Beach, Chlorophyll).
